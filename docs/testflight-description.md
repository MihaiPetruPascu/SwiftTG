SwiftTG is an unofficial Telegram client for iPhone, built from scratch in SwiftUI
on top of TDLib. It aims to be a fast, native, fully accessible way to use
Telegram, with VoiceOver support.

This build covers day-to-day messaging plus voice and video calls, encrypted
group calls, group voice chats and channel live streams, stickers and GIFs, chat
folders, live location sharing, proxies, and two-step verification. You sign in
with your real Telegram phone number; the account and all its data stay on
Telegram's servers.

It is a work in progress. Some areas — group video chats, conference calls, and
live location — have not been fully tested on device yet, so expect rough edges
there. Please report anything that crashes, behaves oddly, reads wrong or is
unreachable with VoiceOver, or looks broken on screen.
