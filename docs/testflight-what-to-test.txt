Some areas are new or not fully tested on device: group video chats, conference calls, and live location. Please report crashes, wrong or unreachable VoiceOver labels, layout that looks broken, and anything that behaves oddly.

Calls
- One-to-one audio and video calls: place, receive, decline, miss; answer from the lock screen; switch audio route (speaker, earpiece, Bluetooth).
- Video: the other person's video appears and keeps updating, your own camera preview is right, front/back switch works, rotating the device reflows both views cleanly.
- Encrypted group calls (conferences): create one from a 1:1 call, add people, everyone sees and hears each other; join from an invitation; leave, rejoin and end without the UI breaking.
- Group voice chats and channel live streams: join, speak, mute and unmute, raise hand, leave; live stream playback starts and stays in sync.
- Screen sharing: start and stop during a call and a conference; it stops the instant you end it.
- Picture in Picture: send a video call to PiP, use other apps, return; the call screen restores with nothing blank or duplicated.
- Call history: the Recent Calls list shows the right people, direction, missed or outgoing, video and conference markers, date and time; the info screen opens.

Messaging
- Stickers: create a set, add, edit, reorder, remove; send them.
- GIFs: search and send, save to favourites, send a saved one; they loop and preview correctly.
- Round video messages: record and send; plays inline, loops, correct thumbnail, audio in sync.
- Chat folders: create, rename, delete, add and remove chats, reorder; the folder tabs match their contents.
- Live location (not fully tested): share it and confirm the other side sees it update as you move and that it stops at expiry or when you stop it; keep moving with the app backgrounded and confirm it still updates.

Sign-in and account
- Verification code screen: it says where the code was sent; Resend counts down then works; "Change Number" goes back with the number kept; Continue is disabled with an empty phone or code; SMS autofill offers the code.
- Two-step verification: set a password, add and verify a recovery email, change and remove it; sign in with 2FA on; "Forgot Password?" with and without a recovery email.
- Log Out (Settings) signs out to the phone-number screen.
- Delete My Account (Settings, Privacy and Security): the confirmation appears; with 2FA on it asks for the password; on success the app returns to login.
- Report a message (message menu) and report a chat or user (Chat Info): the reason list loads from the server, sub-options and the details step work, ending with a confirmation.

Proxy
- Add an MTProto proxy and a SOCKS5 proxy, enable and disable each; chats, media and calls still work with a proxy on.

Notifications
- Push notifications arrive and open the right chat, including when another chat is already open.
- Opening a chat clears its notifications from Notification Centre.
- On launch with a backlog from several chats there is no burst of banners and sounds.
- Check notification sounds, banners, and in-app banners while the app is open.

Layout and accessibility
- Screens lay out correctly across device sizes, orientations and large text: nothing clipped, overlapping or off screen; the message list looks right and scrolls smoothly.
- Full VoiceOver pass: every control reachable and correctly labelled, sensible focus order, no traps; check with Reduce Motion on.

Known issue
- App size grows over time as media (mainly voice messages) is cached. Reduce it in Settings, Data and Storage, Clear Cache; the size figures take a moment to calculate.
